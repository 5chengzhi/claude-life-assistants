# Usefulness review (as of Nov 9, 2026)

Scored by reading each assistant's saved database, not by how impressive the page looks. The question is: **has it changed what I do or saved me money or time?**

Scale: 10 = I'd be worse off without it every week · 7 = clearly useful, with gaps · 5 = useful once · 3 = built but not used.

## Virtual Car (2015 Nissan Rogue): 8/10

**Evidence it's used**
- 40 service records (2015 to Oct 2026), 5 shop visits logged in Sep–Oct 2026 totalling $4,347 in 2026.
- Watch list of 17 issues, with fixed ones moved to "resolved" (alignment, A/C).
- A monthly brief for November, including a plain-language summary in Chinese for family.

**Where it paid off**
- Flagged that the coolant and CVT service was sent to another shop and billed as flushes, not the in-house drain-and-fill that was quoted, and that the fluid brand needs confirming (must be NS-3 or equivalent).
- Caught a tire shop setting all four tires to 38 psi when the spec is 33 cold.
- Caught an invoice odometer reading lower than an earlier shop's.

**Gaps (why not 9)**
- "Trends & costs" has 0 readings (tread depth, battery volts), so the health charts are empty.
- The 24-week learning plan has 0 weeks marked done.
- The monthly brief isn't automated; it exists only when I ask.
- 5 tabs + 6 sub-tabs is more than I open. Inside/Outside/Cabin are reference I visit rarely.

**To reach 9:** log one set of readings at the Dec 5 hood check; schedule the brief for the 1st of each month; either do learning week 1 or cut the Learn tab.

## Wardrobe & Stylist ("Cheng's Closet"): 8/10

**Evidence it's used**
- 99 items catalogued with type, color, formality, season, fit, fabric and care.
- 18 outfits logged from Oct 10 to Nov 9 (about 4 a week), 27 saved outfits, 40 to-dos.
- Saved state rewritten 32 times: it's actively maintained.

**Where it paid off**
- Concrete, repeatable rules from real outfit reviews ("needs a dark anchor", "wear the vest zipped").
- Sorted the closet into wear-now / try-again-later / donate with reasons.

**Gaps (why not 9)**
- 15 of the 18 outfit logs were back-filled by Claude from chat reviews, not entered in the page. The page is the record; chat is the stylist. That's fine, but the in-page log button is barely used.
- Comfort and confidence ratings are empty on every log, and no prices are entered, so cost-per-wear and "what makes me feel good" insights can't work.

**To reach 9:** add a price to each "core" piece; rate comfort and confidence (1–5) on the next 10 outfits.

## Virtual Room: 4/10

**Evidence**
- 0 weekly check-ins, 0 humidity readings, 0 items on the sell list, guest-prep list never used.
- "Where things go" (7 spots) and supplies (8 items) were added on Nov 9, the day of this review.

**Where it paid off**
- The floor plan with collision, door-swing and walking-path checks is the most finished part. Its value is a one-time layout decision, not a weekly one.

**Gaps**
- Every recurring feature depends on a Sunday habit that hasn't started.
- It's too early to judge the new features fairly. This score is "so far", and it's the one most likely to change.

**To reach 7:** do the first check-in this Sunday (one photo from the door, Claude rates it). Four check-ins in a row would show whether it's worth keeping. If not, keep only the Layout tab.

## What separates useful from unused

| | Car | Closet | Room |
|---|---|---|---|
| New data comes from something I already do | Shop visits, receipts | Getting dressed, sending outfit photos | Nothing yet |
| Claude can fill it from a photo in chat | Yes | Yes | Yes, but not done yet |
| Clear payoff each time | Money, avoiding bad repairs | Looking better today | Unclear week to week |

**Lesson for anyone building one:** attach the assistant to something that already happens (a bill, a purchase, getting dressed) and let Claude do the data entry from a photo. Don't rely on remembering a new weekly ritual.
